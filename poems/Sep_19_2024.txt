In the heart of the city where the tall buildings crest,
Lay a story untold, not like all the rest.
It started with taxes, those numbers so cold,
In a world where the young dare to be bold.

There, amidst the ledgers, the nerds found their throne,
With calculators as scepters, in realms of their own.
They danced through the digits, with a peculiar zest,
Turning statutes and codes into a challenging quest.

No tales of whispers, no shadows to cast,
Just the glow of the screens, and the future vast.
In the hum of the press, a unique rhythm was found,
Where finance and dreams were peculiarly bound.

The press, in its wisdom, began to see,
These nerds were not merely as they appeared to be.
Behind thick glasses, and spreadsheets so wide,
Lay adventurers, dreamers, not willing to hide.

So here’s to the nerds, with their taxes in tow,
Building empires from numbers, with a soft, steady glow.
In a world that often overlooks the quiet and the meek,
They press forward, ever strong, uniquely geek.

**Empires of Numbers**
