In lands where belts were masks, not girdles tight,
A lesson lingered in the leather's crease,
Where wisdom wound around waists, silent, bright,
A narrative of strength, a silent peace.

They learned, these bands of bound, enduring skin,
To hold up more than trousers or a sword,
To clasp the essence of what lies within,
A testament to battles, silent, stored.

Upon the ledge of history, they sit,
Observers of the ever-changing dance,
A precipice where past and present meet,
In every crease, a story, not by chance.

No leech to drain the vigor or the life,
But symbiotes in harmony and strife.

Title: "The Quiet Teachers"
