In the still heart where oceans roar and sway,  
A siren's song breathes life into the clay.  
Alone she dances, waves at her command,  
A queen, a deity, untouched by land.  

Beneath the moon's caress, her kingdom wide,  
Where secrets dwell and ancient dreams abide.  
Her voice, a melody that breaks the curse,  
Of silent nights, in her realm, immersed.  

Yet, not all hearts to her sweet call yield,  
A knave, adrift, his spirit unsealed.  
He dances too, but on a different plane,  
A rhythm led by loss, by love, by pain.  

Their worlds apart, yet by fate intertwined,  
A dance of souls, a tale of minds aligned.  
She, in her depth, calls out, a beacon bright,  
He, with his vows, steps forth into the light.  

No whispers here, no shadows dare to creep,  
Between the beats, their promises they keep.  
In every note, a story to be told,  
Of love that's brave, of hearts that turn to gold.  

And so they dance, a spectacle so rare,  
A siren's call, a knave's silent prayer.  
A testament to what can truly be,  
When two worlds meet, in harmony, at sea.

**Title: Harmony at Sea**
