In the archives of minds, where memories recap,
Dwell the moments, leased in time, those rents we tap.
Over past and present, emotions loosely strewn,
A dominion over self, where feelings reign, immune.

Life's cyclical stage, in patterns that rerun,
A tapestry woven, under the same sun.
Each thread interlocking, in destiny's grand loom,
Crafting futures from the past, in time's vast room.

Within this realm of cyclic tales, where history repeats,
Lies the power to alter courses, in defeat’s retreats.
For within the rerun, in the echo of our days,
Exists the chance to amend our ways.

So, let us navigate this maze of time with care,
With eyes wide open, aware of the snare.
For though the past might rerun, in shadows or in light,
Our actions hold the pen, rewriting plights.

Title: "Cycles of Change"
