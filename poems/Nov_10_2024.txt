At the cusp where the sea meets the shore,
There lies Point Denis, with tales of yore.
A miner toiled beneath the earth's crest,
Chasing dreams deeper into the planet's breast.

In his pursuit, within dark veins of coal,
He found not just ore, but the inner soul.
His pickaxe, a brush; the tunnels, his canvas,
He painted his life with each stroke, so endless.

At night, the waves lapped against the sands,
Murmuring secrets from distant lands.
The miner, with hands as rough as the surf,
Wondered about his true place and worth.

Was it beneath, in the cavern's deep heart,
Or here, where the ocean's horizons start?
In silence, he sought what his spirit yearned,
At Point Denis, where the sea churned.

The earth and the ocean, in their embrace,
Taught him of balance, of time and space.
He realized that within and without,
Lies the mine of wisdom, vast and stout.

So here's to the miner, and to Point Denis,
To the quest within us, to find and to free.
For in the depths and the infinite sky,
Lies the essence of you and I.

**Title: "The Miner's Enlightenment at Point Denis"**
