Beneath the azure dome where sunlit echoes roam,  
In the heart of day where goers find their way,  
They tread upon the path, where nature spills her bath,  
A journey, not alone, upon the silent stone.  

Words once softly spoken, like vows never broken,  
Bind the earth to sky, in a bond that never lies.  
Among these gallant trails, beneath the wind's soft wails,  
Lies a truth, pure and bespoke, in every leaf and oak.  

In the air, there's a dance of transient romance,  
A tale of fire's grace, leaving not a single trace.  
Where smoke rises high, painting dreams in the sky,  
A language without speech, where the heavens seek to reach.  

And there, in the midst of all, where the heartbeats echo and call,  
Lies the essence, stark and bright, of life's endless, tireless fight.  
Here, every step divinely sown, under the watchful eyes of the unknown,  
Marks a journey, boldly stroked, among the goers, stone, and smoke.  

**Journey Unspoken**
