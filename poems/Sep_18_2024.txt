Upon the streets where footsteps tread,
Where hopes and dreams are daily fed,
A tale unfolds of voices strong,
Within this verse, they find their song.

In cities vast with stone-paved ways,
Echo sounds of brighter days,
Yet, beneath the sunlit sky,
Riots bloom where passions lie.

With hearts aflame and fists flung high,
Against the silence, they boldly defy,
Charging forth, completely, fully,
In the pursuit of truths unruly.

From the clamor and the clang,
A unified plea began to hang,
Not for the faint, but freedom's choir,
Kindling justice's undying fire.

Together, their spirits never tire,
Their resolve forged in unseen fire,
On battlefields of concrete dreams,
They stitch the world at its frayed seams.

So let this poem be a reminder,
Of the power within each finder,
To challenge, to change, thrumming deeply,
Where paved riots flung fully, freely, steeply.

**Title: "Reverberations on Concrete Dreams"**
