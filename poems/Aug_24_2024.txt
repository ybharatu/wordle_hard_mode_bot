In the twilight of judgement, under the scribe's crimson eye,  
A tableau unfolds, where innocence and accusation lie.  
A tale unfurls, not of whispering trees or shadows deep,  
But of a crime, woven into the fabric of sleep.

A lined face, marked by time,  
In silent testimony to an unnoticed chime.  
Stories etched in furrows deep,  
Secrets, in the heart's keep.

A filet of truth, thinly sliced,  
Served on a platter, where lies are diced.  
A dish of justice, cold and bare,  
In the court's unforgiving glare.

Yet, beneath the veneer of law's cold grace,  
Lies the warmth of mercy, in a softer space.  
For even in judgement, there's a line too thin,  
Between where guilt ends, and innocence begins.

Thus, the scribe pens down, with a steady hand,  
The story of life, in a no-man's land.  
Where crime and virtue, in each other, are filleted,  
And the essence of humanity, in fragile balance, swayed.

**The Essence of Judgement**
