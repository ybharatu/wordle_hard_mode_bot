In the vast, endless dunes, under the eye of a glary sun,  
A camel marches, steady, unfazed, the singular one.  
Its silhouette carving paths where none did run,  
A caravan of one, beneath the same fierce sun.  

In lands far, where green is rare and water dear,  
Hares dart, swift, a flicker of life, then disappear.  
Their thumping feet a rhythm, a dance of primal fear,  
Dodging the cagey predator's gaze, always near.  

And amidst this dance of nature, wild and free,  
Sprouts a young cadet of life, eager to see.  
His eyes alight with dreams of what could be,  
In a world unfettered, boundless, a soul spree.  

For even in the wild, where the camel strides,  
And hares in their burrows snugly hide,  
There's a cadet spirit, that never subsides,  
A lesson in living, in how to abide.  

No cage of circumstance, nor desert vast,  
Can hold the spirit, make it aghast.  
Like the camel, enduring, making its own path,  
And the hares, in their mirth, a reminder to laugh.  

So, from cagey limits, let your heart roam,  
Find your cadet courage, let it be your tome.  
Through deserts or fields, wherever you comb,  
Remember the camel and hare, make this world your home.  

**Sands of Persistence**
