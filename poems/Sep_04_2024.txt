Beneath the Hales, where sea meets spire,
A sturdy ship, with heart and fire,
Cuts through the waves, a path so dire,
Its journey wrought by sheer desire.

Its stern, aglow with morning's light,
Faces the dawn, dispelling night.
Each seaman's eyes, a beacon bright,
Guides this vessel with foresight.

Not a simple trek, nor a jaunt for leisure,
But a voyage filled with measured treasure.
In unity, their efforts measure,
A symphony of toil and pleasure.

Through storms and calm, this crew aspires,
Their spirit, an unquenchable fire.
Each challenge, a rung higher,
On this quest that never tires.

And when at last, the journey's end,
Where ocean's grasp and heavens blend,
They'll find that time, their wounds will mend,
For in their hearts, they've found a friend.

United under the Hales' watchful eye,
Their spirits lifted, ever high,
Towards that towering spire in the sky,
Their memories etched, never to die.

**Voyage of the Spirited Hales**
