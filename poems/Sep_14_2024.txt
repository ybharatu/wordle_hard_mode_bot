Under the azure expanse where eagles dare,
There danced sixes, unfurling in the air.
Each figure, a grace, a story untold,
In the land where bravery meets the bold.

With talon sharp, and gaze fixed tight,
A cobra slithered out of night.
Its broad back gleaming under sun’s fiery kiss,
A serpentine elegance, venomous bliss.

From far off lands where wild hearts roam,
Came a bravo, his spirit, his home.
With courage nestled in the crook of his smile,
He wandered through life's vast, unending mile.

Together they danced, a mosaic of dream,
Under a sky where the stars did gleam.
Sixes and talon, cobra with pride,
Bravo walked forward, with wide steps, wide.

Through the dance of chaos, of wild, of free,
They found harmony, a rare decree.
In a world spun from the threads of fate,
They stood tall, together, against the gate.

So sings the earth, of sixes in flight,
Of talon sharp, under the moon’s light.
Of cobra’s dance, in the warmth of the brave,
A tale woven, from the cradle to the grave.

**A Tapestry of Dreams**
